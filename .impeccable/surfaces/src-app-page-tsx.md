# Landing page Aurum Eleven

Scope: the single public page (`src/app/page.tsx`) and its form endpoint. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: event managers, HR and marketing teams and event agencies booking music for a gala, ball or large production; they need a safe choice and a fast quote. Action: send the availability enquiry (name, e-mail, optional phone, message). Proof on hand: 12 documentary photos, six re-shot line-up photos (2026-11-18), 20 s silent showreel, the full recording on YouTube, credits listed in PRODUCT.md. Copy is the client's STRUKTURA PDF plus the corrections PDF of 2026-11-18 ("POPRAWKA WWW"), verbatim.

Constraints: CTA in hero, after "Co dostajesz", after the live film, after "Kto stoi na scenie" (those three centred, per the client), and above the form; pinned enquiry button on phones. Form sends through Resend when `RESEND_API_KEY` and `CONTACT_TO_EMAIL` exist; otherwise it says plainly that sending is not enabled yet and shows the alternative contact. Remotion renders the hero loop. The live section embeds the client's full recording with sound (UPvItS4fOE0, youtube-nocookie, loaded only on click, starting at 10:38, under the graded LED-wall photo of the vocalists); the hero's "Zobacz pełne nagranie" opens the same recording on YouTube. The silent Remotion line-up film stays in video/ for social use. Each documentary picture is used once: the whole band belongs to the hero stage, the horn section, percussion, vocalists and sound engineer (client photos 3–6) illustrate "Co dostajesz", the LED-wall vocalists are the recording's poster; "Jak gramy" is text only so the answer follows the problem at once. Nav labels: Jak gramy?, Oferta, Na żywo, O nas, Współpraca, FAQ. Deploy to a separate Vercel dev project.

Unresolved: business e-mail, phone, company data, privacy policy. (The stage-plan schematic was removed at the client's request, 2026-11-18.)

Corrections of 2026-11-18 (applied 2026-11-19, all but point 1 and the stage lettering, which a separate session owns): YouTube link and no full stop in the overline; the facts as a gold-ruled band of four with gold line icons; "Największy problem eventów" as the problem heading's lead line in sentence case; horn photo out of "Jak gramy"; schematic removed; line icons and photos 3–6 in "Co dostajesz"; LED-wall photo as the recording's poster; live header centred on one line, recording from 10:38; line-up re-shot photos with pillars that open on hover or tap; centred section CTAs; new nav labels; footer line "11 muzyków. Jedna scena. Własne aranżacje."; more gold, as the Beam Rule in DESIGN.md records.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The lit stage: the showreel fills the first screen in monochrome, and colour lights it wherever the visitor points. Eleven musicians, one signature sound. It refuses the category default: a full-bleed party hero under a dark wash with the headline on the footage, gold-on-black "premium" chrome, logo walls and stat counters.

OWN-WORLD: Matte black #111111 and ivory #F5F2EC own alternating sections; satin gold #B08D57 behaves like the logo's beam: at the culmination (the lit pillar of the line-up, the final process numeral, button hover) and, since 2026-11-19 at the client's request, as a few drawn accents at rest (the hero facts band's rule and icons, "Dla kogo" checks, FAQ plus/minus, process dots), never a field, heading or body text. Graphite #2B2B2B rules. Inter Tight Light display set large and tight, Manrope for text, square edges, ivory buttons on black and black on ivory, no arrows, no tracked capitals outside the logotype. Documentary photography in the warm held colour (the user found the near-monochrome grade gloomy, 2026-11-17).

STORY: The organiser recognises the empty-dancefloor risk, sees the whole stage and how the band reads the guests, learns exactly what they get and how the process runs, sees who stands on stage, and asks about a date.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black header, then the living stage filling the rest of the screen edge to edge (100svh − 72px, at least 440px): the showreel drawn live in monochrome with a soft pool of the warm held colour following the pointer (drifting on its own on touch), and a square pause control at its bottom right. No pillars, gaps or steps: the user asked for "full wysokość i bez pasków" (2026-11-17). Below the fold the sentence-case overline "Zespół na event firmowy" (no full stop, per the client) and the H1 in light display, left-aligned; then the lead beside the ivory primary button "Zapytaj o dostępność terminu" with its microcopy and the play link, which opens the full recording on YouTube; then the facts band (four cells under a 2px gold rule, each with a gold line icon) and the credits. The header's own enquiry button keeps a call to action in the first viewport; the pinned phone button waits until most of the stage has scrolled away (user: "żeby nie było od razu zapytaj o dostępność terminu", 2026-11-17).

FORM: Code-led; eleven pillars, the canvas direction carried into code. seed: none (pinned). No roll ran: the world is the eleven-pillar Claude Design canvas the user approved ("wydaje mi się, że idziemy w dobrym kierunku"), and the hero is the specific direction the user picked by name from a described set ("Overdrive tutaj": the showreel drawn live by a shader through eleven pillars, monochrome with colour following the cursor and drifting on its own on phones, the gold centre kept, the pre-rendered video as the fallback). Signature interaction: the living stage. On load the pillars rise from both edges toward the centre, the gold pillar last; the footage plays inside them in a calm slow-motion cut; the light drifts slowly across the pillars while nobody steers it. That stage is the only unprompted motion on the page, it has a visible pause control (remembered per visitor) and is a still frame under reduced motion. Motion that answers the visitor: colour following the pointer over the stage, colour lighting one pillar of the line-up in "Kto stoi na scenie" (the user picked "Piony, kolor na dotyk" from three described directions), FAQ expand, form confirmation. On 2026-11-17 the user tried four replacements in turn (pillars closing into one warm picture, "Jeden z jedenastu" opened by scroll, a plain video with no script, then the closed pillars again) and came back to this lit stage: "do tego co się podświetlało na webgl". Photography elsewhere stays in the warm held colour. Then, the same day: "full wysokość i bez pasków" — the stage now fills the screen with no pillars, and the light that lit one pillar became a round pool of colour that follows the pointer in both axes.
